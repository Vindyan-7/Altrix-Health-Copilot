## How to read a report
- Preserve the report's exact value, unit, date, and laboratory reference interval.
- If no interval is present, do not invent one.
- Reference intervals can vary by laboratory and relevant patient factors.
- A result outside a reference interval does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

## Questions a user may ask a clinician
- What does this result mean in the context of my health?
- Do I need repeat testing or additional evaluation?
- Are there factors that could affect this result?
