# Understanding medical records

A medical report records information from a particular test, visit, or episode of care. It should be read with its date, units, reference ranges, and clinical context.

The copilot can explain terminology and organize information, but it cannot determine what a result means for a particular person on its own. If a result is unclear, unexpected, or concerning, discuss it with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Product rules
- Always distinguish the report's wording from general explanation.
- Preserve units and dates.
- Do not compare measurements when units or methods are incompatible.
- Do not imply that an out-of-range value is a diagnosis.
